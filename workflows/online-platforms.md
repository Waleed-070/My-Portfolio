## Leonardo.ai (Recommended)

### Account Setup
1. **Sign up:** [leonardo.ai](https://leonardo.ai)
2. **Free tier:** 150 daily credits
3. **Premium:** $12/month for unlimited credits

### ROBOT-MOTHER Generation Steps

#### Step 1: Choose Model
- **Photorealistic** (for realistic android)
- **Cinematic** (for dramatic lighting)
- **Absolute Reality** (for high detail)
- **Leonardo Diffusion XL** (for balanced results)

#### Step 2: Input Settings
**Prompt:**
```
cinematic portrait of ROBOT-MOTHER, serene female android, face sculpted from polished chrome and brushed aluminum, intricate bioluminescent circuit patterns glowing soft cyan and magenta light, translucent pearlescent synth-skin, hair made of flowing fiber-optic filaments shimmering with data, minimalist futuristic temple setting, holographic data streams floating geometric shapes, art deco aesthetics fused with cybernetics, hyper-detailed, insanely detailed, photorealistic, volumetric lighting, cinematic, 8k resolution, shot on Arri Alexa, professional photography, masterpiece, ultra-realistic
```

**Negative Prompt:**
```
cartoon, anime, painting, drawing, illustration, low quality, blurry, pixelated, distorted face, malformed hands, ugly, deformed, artificial skin, plastic, toy, mannequin, watermark, text, signature
```

#### Step 3: Generation Parameters
- **AI Image Generation:** Selected
- **Quantity:** 4 images
- **Dimensions:** 1024x576 (16:9 ratio)
- **Guidance Scale:** 7.5
- **Step Count:** 15-25
- **Tiling:** Off
- **PhotoReal:** On (if available)
- **Alchemy:** On (for premium users)
- **Prompt Magic:** V3 (if available)

#### Step 4: Advanced Options
- **Scheduler:** DPM++ 2M Karras or Euler a
- **Seed:** -1 for random
- **Init Strength:** N/A (text to image)
- **Presets:** Cinematic or Photorealistic

#### Step 5: Post-Processing
1. **Select best image** from generated batch
2. **Use "Unzoom"** for upscaling (if available)
3. **Download** as PNG for maximum quality

### Pro Tips for Leonardo.ai
- **Use PhotoReal toggle** for enhanced realism
- **Enable Alchemy** for higher quality (premium)
- **Adjust Guidance Scale** between 7-8 for balanced results
- **Use step count 20-25** for optimal detail

## Platform-Specific Troubleshooting

### Leonardo.ai Issues
- **Low quality:** Enable PhotoReal and Alchemy
- **Distorted faces:** Lower guidance scale to 7.0
- **Slow generation:** Use smaller initial resolution

### DreamStudio Issues
- **Cost concerns:** Limit steps to 25-30
- **Slow processing:** Use queue for overnight generation
- **Quality variance:** Adjust prompt strength

### SeaArt.ai Issues
- **Credit limits:** Use free tier strategically
- **Inconsistent results:** Try different models
- **Quality caps:** Upgrade to premium for best results

### Mage.space Issues
- **Server overload:** Try off-peak hours
- **Limited features:** Consider premium upgrade
- **Variable quality:** Use multiple generations
