## Prompt Weighting Strategies

### Emphasis Techniques
Use parentheses for emphasis:
- `(ROBOT-MOTHER:1.3)` - Strong character focus
- `(bioluminescent circuits:1.2)` - Enhanced circuit visibility
- `(cinematic lighting:1.15)` - Stronger lighting effect

### Negative Weighting
Use brackets for reduction:
- `[cartoon]` - Reduce cartoon elements
- `[painting]` - Avoid painterly style
- `[blurry]` - Prevent blur
